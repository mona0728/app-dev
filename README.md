# My Favorite Movie: Inside Out 2 🎬
## About the Movie
**Inside Out 2** is an animated movie by Disney and Pixar. It shows the different emotions inside a young girl's mind as she experiences new changes in her life.
## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because it teaches us that all emotions are important. It reminds me that it is okay to feel sad, happy, scared, or confused.
## My Favorite Characters
- **Joy** – Always tries to make things better.
- **Sadness** – Teaches us that it is okay to feel sad.
- **Anxiety** – Shows how we sometimes worry too much.
- **Anger** – Represents our frustrations.
## Lessons I Learned
1. Accept your feelings.
2. Do not be afraid of changes.
3. Every emotion has a purpose.
4. Love and accept yourself.
